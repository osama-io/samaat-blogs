Hearing loss affects millions of people across Pakistan, yet many suffer in silence due to lack of awareness or stigma surrounding hearing aids. Early diagnostic tests and modern digital hearing aids help restore clarity, confidence, and quality of life.

## Types of Hearing Loss

Understanding the root cause is the first step toward effective treatment. Hearing loss generally falls into three main categories:

- **Sensorineural Hearing Loss:** Caused by damage to inner ear hair cells or auditory nerves. Commonly due to aging, continuous loud noise exposure, or genetics.
- **Conductive Hearing Loss:** Occurs when sound wave transmission is blocked in the outer or middle ear (e.g., earwax blockage, fluid, or middle ear infections).
- **Mixed Hearing Loss:** A combination of sensorineural and conductive factors.

## How Audiograms Work

An audiogram is a standardized graph illustrating a person's hearing threshold across frequencies (from 250 Hz to 8000 Hz). Qualified audiologists perform pure-tone audiometry to measure softest audible sounds in decibels (dB), guiding accurate hearing aid fitting.
