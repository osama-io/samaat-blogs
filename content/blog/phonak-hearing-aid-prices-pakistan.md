Phonak hearing aid price in Pakistan in 2026 ranges from about PKR 60,000 per ear for entry Audéo models to PKR 550,000+ for the flagship Audéo Infinio Sphere. Most Phonak buyers in Pakistan land in the PKR 120,000–250,000 band per ear, where the Audéo Lumity and Life lines deliver the brand's signature speech-in-noise performance.

Phonak sits at the premium end of the Pakistani market. What you pay extra for is the AutoSense OS sound-scene classification, Roger microphone compatibility, and the broadest pediatric range (Sky) of any brand in the country.

Per-pair, expect double the above. Fitting and trial follow-ups are usually included at dealers and audiology clinics; always confirm in writing.

## What moves a Phonak price up or down

- **Platform generation**: an outgoing Lumity costs noticeably less than Infinio with similar daily benefit — the best value window is the last months of a platform's life.
- **Receiver vs custom**: Virto customs add ear-impression and shell costs on top.
- **Charger and power**: rechargeable lithium models run higher, but kill battery-consumable costs by year two.
- **Roger receivers/microphones**: education and workplace add-ons are priced separately — quote them explicitly if you need them.

## Is Phonak worth the premium over Signia or Rexton?

Roughly: Rexton and Signia deliver 90% of the everyday benefit at 60–70% of Phonak's price. Phonak earns its premium where it matters most — meeting rooms, classrooms (Roger), severe-profound losses, and pediatric fittings. Buy Phonak for those cases; buy Signia/Rexton for value-first fittings. ## Buying a genuine Phonak in Pakistan

1. Ask for the serial number and warranty registration (2–3 years by tier, app-verified via myPhonak).
2. Get the model name and number in writing — "Lumity 90" and "Lumity 50" differ by tens of thousands of rupees.
3. Confirm the charger is included; some quotes price it separately.
4. Ask how many fine-tuning sessions the price covers and within what window.
