Signia Pure hearing aid price in Pakistan starts at roughly Rs 85,000 per ear for the Pure 312 1X entry tier and reaches Rs 285,000-380,000 for the Pure 7X. Pure is Signia's RIC (receiver-in-canal) family - a small behind-the-ear shell with the receiver on a wire inside the canal, popular with buyers who want discretion without going full invisible.

## What Signia Pure Is - and Why RIC Suits Pakistani Buyers

The Pure family is Signia's flagship receiver-in-canal line. Instead of pushing all sound through a tube like a traditional BTE, Pure sits a slim battery-and-processor unit behind the ear and runs a thin wire to a receiver speaker placed directly in the canal. Three practical benefits:

- **Discretion**: the wire is nearly invisible under most hairstyles, far more so than a tube-and-mold BTE.
- **Sound quality**: the receiver in the canal gives cleaner high-frequency delivery, which matters most for theCommon symptom in Pakistan - moderate-to-high-frequency loss from years of bazaar noise, traffic, generators.
- **Comfort**: lighter on the ear for long wear in heat than a full mold.

Across Signia's whole range in Pakistan, prices run Rs 55,000-380,000 per ear; Pure occupies the upper half of that band because it is the flagship shape, not the budget one.

## Battery vs Rechargeable Pure

Pure has two main power options in Pakistan:

| Variant | Power | Rough price band per ear |
| :--- | --- | :--- |
| Pure 312 (battery) | Zinc-air size 312 (brown), 5-7 days per battery | 1X from about Rs 85,000 up through tiers |
| Pure rechargeable | Built-in lithium, charge overnight | Premium of the battery equivalent, dealer-dependent |

Battery care is easy and cheap: size 312 zinc-air batteries run Rs 350-650 per six-pack, and the brown 312 size is one of the most widely stocked sizes in Pakistan's pharmacy and clinic network. Rechargeable Pure removes weekly battery handling but adds charger logistics, and for travel or village use where stable overnight electricity is uncertain, the 312 battery version remains the more forgiving choice.

## Pure vs Styletto vs Motion - Which Signia Shape Do You Want?

Signia sells more than Pure in Pakistan, and it pays to know where Pure sits among siblings:

| Family | Shape | Who it suits |
| :--- | --- | :--- |
| Pure | Slim RIC on a wire | Anyone wanting discretion with moderate-high frequency loss |
| Styletto | Elongated RIC, more fashion-forward, larger battery | Buyers who want a distinct design and longer wear per charge |

## Where Pure Is Cheapest in Pakistan

Lahore is the densest distributor market for Signia in Pakistan, and mid-range prices there run 5-10% cheaper than in Karachi, Islamabad, or less-served cities. On a Rs 285,000-380,000 Pure 7X, that is roughly Rs 15,000-35,000 of difference between the same tier quoted in Lahore versus further out. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Signia Pure the same as Silk?

No. Pure is a receiver-in-canal (RIC) device with a unit behind the ear and a receiver on a wire in the canal. Silk is Signia's fully-in-canal "instant-fit" device that sits entirely inside the ear. Pure is more common in Pakistan and more flexible; Silk is smaller. Prices differ accordingly, with Pure generally the wider and better-supported range here.

### How does Pure compare to Motion?

Motion is Signia's BTE (behind-the-ear) family, a larger shell with more power and larger controls. Pure is slimmer and more discreet but with somewhat less raw power ceiling. For severe loss or users who need a very robust shell, Motion is the safer choice; for moderate-to-high frequency loss where discretion matters, Pure is the better match.

### What does the rechargeable Pure cost in Pakistan?

Rechargeable Pure carries a premium over the equivalent 312 battery model, and exact figures vary dealer to dealer. The 312 battery tier is well defined: Pure 312 1X from about Rs 85,000 per ear up to Pure 312 7X at Rs 285,000-380,000 per ear. Ask your clinic to quote both before committing, since rechargeable stock is thinner in Pakistan and prices move accordingly.

### Is the 5X worth it over the 3X?

For most Pakistani buyers with mild-to-moderate loss, the jump from 3X to 5X is incremental rather than transformative - better noise handling and a few more channels. It is worth the upgrade when speech-in-noise is genuinely hard for you (shops, offices, classrooms) or your audiogram is steep. Below that, the 3X will often sound nearly the same for a substantial saving.

### Where in Pakistan is Signia Pure cheapest?

Lahore, because it is the densest distributor market for Signia, with mid-range prices running 5-10% cheaper than Karachi or Islamabad. On the Pure 312 7X at Rs 285,000-380,000 per ear, that can be a meaningful saving - but only shift the purchase if you also have a plan for servicing and fittings near home, since warranty runs 12-24 months manufacturer.
