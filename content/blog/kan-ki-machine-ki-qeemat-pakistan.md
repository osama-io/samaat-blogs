## Sasti machine se kya hota hai, asli se kya

Bazaar mein aap ko PKR 3,000 se 5,000 mein "hearing aid" bhi mil jayega, lekin woh amplifier hota hai, hearing aid nahi. Farq yeh hai:

1. **Amplifier** sab kuch barha deta hai, shor aur awaz dono, koi aap ki sunne ki wilayat (audiogram) ke sath tune nahi hota.
2. **Genuine hearing aid** aap ki audiogram ke hisab se program ki jati hai, shor kam karta hai, aur acchi awaz deta hai.

A&M aur Interton ka edge yehi hai: qeemat kam, lekin audiogram ke hisab se tune hoti hain, is liye asli zindagi mein faida deti hain. Sirf sasta na dekhein; sunne mein farq dekhein.
