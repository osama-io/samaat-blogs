> Updated September 2026. Prices per ear, Pakistan market, genuine imported stock.

Hearing aid types and costs in Pakistan: behind-the-ear (BTE) and receiver-in-canal (RIC) styles run PKR 15,000–350,000 per ear, custom in-ear styles (ITE, ITC, CIC, IIC) run PKR 25,000–550,000. The smaller and more invisible the style, the higher the premium for the same technology level.

Know the style you need before you start shopping for brands — it is the first filter, and it changes the price more than most features do.

## All hearing aid types with Pakistan costs (2026)

| Style | Visibility | Price per ear (PKR) | Best for | Watch out for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| BTE (behind-the-ear) | Visible | 15,000 – 200,000 | All losses incl. severe; children; easiest service | Cosmetics; feedback if poorly fitted |
| RIC / RITE | Subtle | 25,000 – 350,000 | Most adults; best size-performance balance | Receiver (inner part) needs periodic cleaning/replacement |

## How style and price interact

Two rules of thumb for 2026:

1. **Discretion is taxed.** For the same platform tier, in-canal customs cost 10–25% more than an equivalent RIC, plus ear-impression and remake costs.
2. **Features need space.** Bluetooth antennas, rechargeable cells and telecoils live in BTE/RIC housings. A CIC at the "same tier" always has fewer features than a BTE at the same price.

If you do not specifically need invisibility, RIC is the default recommendation in Pakistan: most fittings per rupee, easiest maintenance, widest model choice. ## Frequently asked questions

**What are the main hearing aid types and costs in Pakistan?**
Six styles: BTE (PKR 15,000+), RIC (25,000+), ITE (30,000+), ITC (40,000+), CIC (50,000+), IIC (80,000+) per ear, scaling up to PKR 200,000–550,000 at premium technology tiers. RIC is the most popular balance of price, features and discretion.

**Which hearing aid type is cheapest in Pakistan?**
BTE — its larger housing fits cheaper components, needs no custom shell, and is cheapest to service. Entry BTE digital devices start around PKR 15,000 per ear.

**Which type is best for severe hearing loss?**
BTE or high-power RIC. Canal styles physically cannot house the receivers and ventilation severe losses need.

**Do invisible hearing aids cost more?**
Yes — typically 10–25% over an equivalent RIC, plus custom-mold fees. Compare tiers honestly in the [invisible guide](/blog/invisible-hearing-aids-pakistan-cic-iic-guide).
