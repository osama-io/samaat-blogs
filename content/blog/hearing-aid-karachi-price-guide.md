Hearing aid price in Karachi in 2026 ranges from PKR 15,000 per ear for entry digital devices to PKR 550,000+ for premium AI-platform flagships. ## Where to buy in Karachi

Audiology clinics in Clifton, DHA and Gulshan, and the long-established medical devices market around Saddar. For first-time fittings choose a clinic with an in-house audiologist and a sound booth; for a known model, distributor counters price sharper but rarely include fine-tuning sessions. Karachi's traffic makes the follow-up count worth negotiating — devices needing frequent in-person adjustments cost you time as well as rupees.

## Keeping Karachi quotes honest

1. Insist on the exact model number and platform tier in writing.
2. Confirm per-ear vs per-pair pricing (both conventions circulate here).
3. Verify warranty registration and where the service happens in Karachi.
4. Count the included follow-ups — this is where Karachi quotes hide 15–20%.
