> Updated September 2026 with current Islamabad/Rawalpindi quotes. Prices per ear.

Hearing aid price in Islamabad and Rawalpindi in 2026 ranges from PKR 15,000 per ear for entry digital devices to PKR 550,000+ for flagship platforms. The twin cities have Pakistan's strongest concentration of qualified audiologists — several clinics offer Real Ear Measurement (REM) verification fitting, the gold standard — so the premium here is small and the fitting quality is often the best in the country.

## Islamabad / Rawalpindi price bands (2026)

| Tier | Price per ear (PKR) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Entry digital | 15,000 – 45,000 | Basic BTE, Interton/Rexton entry |
| Mid-range | 70,000 – 160,000 | Signia Pure, Rexton biX, Widex Sheer |
| Upper-mid | 160,000 – 250,000 | Phonak Lumity, Styletto, Moment 330 |
| Premium | 300,000 – 550,000+ | Phonak Infinio Sphere, Signia IX |

A mid-range pair, fitted with follow-ups, typically totals **PKR 140,000–300,000** across the twin cities.

## Where to buy in the twin cities

Audiology clinics in F-sectors (Islamabad) and Saddar/Bank Road (Rawalpindi) carry Signia, Phonak, Widex and Rexton stock. Military-area referrals (MH/CMH audiology departments) feed much of the market — Many clinics there serve armed-forces families and maintain solid import channels. For first-time buyers: the clinics that advertise REM verification are the ones that measure whether the device actually delivers prescribed amplification in your ear — worth the location.

## Twin-cities quote checklist

1. Exact model number and platform tier, in writing.
2. REM verification included or extra? (Some slabs charge PKR 5,000–15,000.)
3. Per ear or per pair.
4. Follow-up count and validity window.
5. Warranty registration and Islamabad service point.
