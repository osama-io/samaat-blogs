> Updated September 2026. We checked dozens of Daraz and OLX hearing-aid listings against clinic and distributor stock.

Hearing aid price in Pakistan on Daraz runs from about PKR 1,500 for amplifier-style "hearing aids" to PKR 80,000+ for a handful of genuine entry devices. On OLX you mostly find second-hand clinic devices and gray-market imports. Neither platform sells the mid and premium range that fits most hearing losses — that is a dealer and clinic product here, for warranty and programming reasons.

## What Daraz listings actually are

| Price on Daraz (PKR) | What it is | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1,500 – 5,000 | Sound amplifiers (PSAP), some falsely listed as "hearing aids" | Generic amplification, no audiogram tuning; okay as a temporary TV booster, not a hearing aid |
| 8,000 – 25,000 | Entry digital devices, mostly Chinese platforms | Real but basic; verify programmable fitting is even possible |
| 40,000 – 90,000 | Occasionally genuine Rexton/Interton entry models | Only safe with serial-number warranty verification |

**The giveaway:** real hearing aids require programming to your audiogram and a local warranty service point. A listing that ships in a box with no mention of fitting, brand warranty registration, or the seller's audiology credentials is an amplifier or a gray import — whatever the images show.

## What OLX listings actually are

Mostly resold second-hand devices: used aids outlive their fit (hearing changes, devices are programmed to the original owner's audiogram, domes/receivers age). Refurbished clinical devices at 40–60% of retail can be a legitimate deal for a knowledgable buyer working with their own fitter — but "programmed to someone else's ear" is the default outcome, not the exception.

## Price comparison on the same tier

Same tier of mid-range device (Signia/Rexton, 8–16 channel):

- **Daraz gray import:** PKR 45,000–60,000 — no warranty service, no fitting.
- **Authorized dealer:** PKR 70,000–110,000 — warranty 1–2 years, fitting, follow-ups.
- **OLX used:** PKR 30,000–50,000 — programmed to the previous owner.

The PKR 25,000–40,000 you "save" at the marketplace is precisely the fitting, tuning and warranty that determine whether the device works for your ear at all.

## When buying online in Pakistan is safe

1. You already have an audiogram and a model recommendation.
2. The seller is an authorized dealer with a registered warranty (not a marketplace reseller).
3. Fitting/programming is included or arranged.

That describes a dealer or clinic rather than Daraz/OLX — which is why our catalog lives at samaat.pk with WhatsApp-based quoting, and why audiogram-first posts like the [full price guide](/blog/hearing-aid-prices-in-pakistan-2026) always precede any purchase advice.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the hearing aid price in Pakistan on Daraz?**
PKR 1,500–90,000. Below PKR 8,000 the listings are amplifiers, not hearing aids. The few genuine devices on Daraz are entry-tier; mid and premium devices are not sold there with valid warranties.

**Are Daraz hearing aids genuine?**
Sometimes — but only the PKR 40,000+ listings from authorized sellers, verifiable via serial-number warranty registration. Below that, most "hearing aids" are PSAP amplifiers.

**Is buying a used hearing aid on OLX a good idea?**
Rarely. Devices are programmed to the previous owner's audiogram, receivers age, and there is no warranty transfer in Pakistan. Only worthwhile if your own audiologist programs and services it.
