Hearing aid repair cost in Pakistan runs Rs 5,000-15,000 for typical minor out-of-warranty fixes like a receiver swap or moisture clean, with official brand-level service charging more. Manufacturer warranty lasts 12-24 months; after that, the repair-versus-replace decision depends on the device's age against the 5-7 years most aids stay usable here.

## The Warranty Landscape in Pakistan

Almost every genuine hearing aid sold in Pakistan - imported or made in Pakistan - ships with a manufacturer warranty of **12 to 24 months**. The warranty period depends on brand and tier; premium models from Phonak, Oticon, Signia generally sit at the 24-month end, while entry imported devices often carry 12 months. The warranty is registered against the serial number at the point of fitting, so insist that the clinic writes it down and gives you the warranty card or receipt with the serial number.

What the warranty usually covers: manufacturing defects, receiver failure, microphone failure, and shell or housing problems that were not caused by the user. What it does not cover: loss, water damage that gets in through misuse, broken battery doors from forcing, and anything the user opened themselves.

## What Voids the Warranty

Voiding is not theoretical here. The three fastest ways Pakistani buyers flip a working warranty card into a dead one:

| Action | Result |
| :--- | --- |
| Getting it opened/repaired at a non-authorised shop | Usually voids warranty, even if that shop fixed it fine |
| Sweat and moisture ingress | Often not covered, since moisture damage from wear is treated as user-side |
| Serial number tampering (sticker removed, shell repainted) | Warranty claim rejected outright |

Sweat being listed above is the honest, uncomfortable part: in Pakistan heat, sweat damage is one of the most common warranty disputes. Asking the clinic at purchase to state, in writing, how sweat-related faults are treated saves months of argument later. A [consistent daily care routine](/blog/how-to-care-for-your-hearing-aids) is not just hygiene - it is warranty insurance.

## Out-of-Warranty Repair Costs

Once a device is outside its 12-24 month window, these are the realistic numbers quoted by dealers and clinics:

| Repair type | Typical cost (PKR) | Turnaround |
| :--- | --- | :--- |
| Minor moisture clean and seal | 5,000-8,000 | 3-7 days |
| Receiver/speaker replacement | 8,000-15,000 | 5-10 days |
| Microphone replacement | 8,000-15,000 | 5-10 days |
| Shell crack repair or recasing | 10,000-15,000 | 7-14 days |
| Major internal fault via official brand service | Above the minor band, quote-based | 2-4 weeks, parts often imported |

Those numbers are for "typical minor" work. Official brand service centres (i.e. channels tied to a specific brand like Signia, Oticon or Phonak) run higher and slower because parts come from abroad. Local dealer network repairs are faster and cheaper but vary in quality - a good dealer has a working receiver stock and gets a device back in days; a bad one sends everything to Karachi or Lahore and tells you to wait.

## Dealer vs Bazaar Repair Shops

Pakistan has both, and the difference matters more out of warranty than in.

| Channel | Pros | Cons |
| :--- | --- | :--- |
| Authorised dealer/clinic service | Serial-tracked, genuine parts, WARRANTY not voided by future claims, programming retained | Costlier, slower on odd parts |
| Bazaar repair shop | Cheapest for a quick fix, sometimes same-day | Often re-sells amplifier-grade parts, no warranty protection, can brick a programmable device |

For a device under the 5-7 year mark that you plan to keep wearing, authorised dealer service is nearly always the right call - the extra Rs 3,000-5,000 is insurance against a loaner-grade fix that makes the device unreliable for the remaining years of its usable life. ## Repair or Replace: the 5-7 Year Decision Point

Every hearing aid in Pakistan has a realistic usable life of 5-7 years; heat, humidity and sweat shorten it compared to the 7-10 years often quoted abroad. Repair-versus-replace is really a question of where on that clock the device sits.

| Device age | Repair worth it? |
| :--- | --- |
| 0-2 years | Yes, almost always - often under warranty anyway |
| 2-4 years | Usually yes, if repair is under Rs 15,000 and the device still suits your hearing |
| 4-6 years | Borderline - repair if minor, replace if the fault is a receiver or microphone |
| 6+ years | Almost always replace; repair money is better spent as down payment |

The maths matters because prices of replacements shifted: A&M genuine aids start at Rs 8,000-35,000 per ear, and good mid-range imported devices at Rs 25,000-180,000. Spending Rs 12,000 to keep a 7-year-old device alive is often Rs 12,000 toward a new one that also picks up newer fittings and better noise management. If your hearing test too has moved (get a fresh PTA - Rs 500-2,000), the old device may simply no longer have enough range to be reprogrammed usefully, which is a replace case even with a healthy shell.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is repairing an old hearing aid worth it?

Usually not past 5-7 years. A Rs 10,000 repair on a device that only has a year of reliable life left is false economy; the same Rs 10,000 is better as part of a replacement, since genuine A&M starts at Rs 8,000-35,000 per ear and mid-range imports at Rs 25,000-180,000.

### How long does a hearing aid warranty last in Pakistan?

12-24 months manufacturer, depending on brand and tier. Premium lines like Phonak, Oticon and Signia generally sit at the top of that band. The warranty is registered against the serial number at fitting, so get it in writing and keep the receipt.

### Does sweating void the warranty?

Moisture damage from sweat is frequently treated as user-side rather than manufacturing defect, so it can cause a warranty denial even within the 12-24 month window. Ask the selling clinic to state their sweat-damage policy in writing before you buy, and use a dry box (Rs 1,500-4,000) nightly.

### Can any shop repair a hearing aid?

Physically, yes - bazaar repair shops are common and cheap. But they void future warranty claims, often fit amplifier-grade parts to programmable devices, and keep no serial record. For any device still within warranty or under about 5 years old, use an authorised dealer or brand service centre.

### How long does a typical repair take?

Minor moisture cleans and receiver swaps at a good dealer run 3-10 days. Major internal faults at official brand service can take 2-4 weeks because parts are imported. Ask for a written estimate of both cost and turnaround before handing over the device, and insist on a receipt noting the serial number.
