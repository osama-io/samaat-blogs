## Daraz vs dealer: the quality trap

Daraz and OLX listings for hearing aid accessories are cheap for honest reasons and dishonest ones:

- **What's fine on Daraz:** dry boxes, cleaning tools, storage cases. Generic hardware with nothing to calibrate.
- **What's risky:** zinc-air batteries. Counterfeit and expired cells are common — they die in days instead of the size's rated lifespan, and a leaking cell corrodes the contact and can contribute to real damage. Buy batteries from a dealer or an established pharmacy brand you recognise.
- **What's a false economy:** cheap "universal" earmolds and retention cords that don't match your aid's acoustics. A bad earmold seal undoes the aid's programming.

Rule of thumb: consumables that touch the aid's electronics (batteries, wax guards) come from the dealer; consumables that just touch the aid's body (dry box, cord, brush) can come from anywhere. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do rechargeable hearing aids still need accessories?

Yes — fewer, but not zero. You still need a dry box (rechargeables sweat too), wax guards, cleaning tools, and earmolds if you use a custom dome. What you avoid is the Rs 3,000–8,000 yearly battery bill, offset by an internal battery-pack replacement after 3–4 years of daily charging.

### Are Daraz hearing aid batteries safe to buy?

Mixed. Dry boxes and cleaning tools are fine; zinc-air batteries are the risky buy, because counterfeit and expired cells are widespread and they die in days or leak onto the contacts. Buy batteries from a hearing aid dealer or a recognised pharmacy chain. The Rs 350–650 per 6-pack price at a dealer includes genuine stock.

### How long does a hearing aid earmold last?

One to two years for an adult before the seal degrades enough to whistle or leak; six to twelve months for a young child whose ear canal is still growing. Each remake costs Rs 2,000–5,000 and needs a fresh ear impression.

### What accessories come free with a hearing aid in Pakistan?

It varies by dealer, but the standard bundle is typically the required batteries, a basic cleaning tool, a carry case and sometimes a starter set of wax guards — a dry box is sometimes thrown in with mid- and premium-level purchases. Ask for the exact list in writing before paying; the first-year accessory bill adds Rs 7,000–20,000 per ear if you buy everything separately.

### What's the total yearly running cost of a hearing aid?

For one battery-powered ear, roughly Rs 7,000–20,000 in year one (which includes the dry box and earmold) and Rs 5,000–16,000 in ongoing years. Two ears roughly double it. Rechargeable two-ear setups run about Rs 5,000–16,000 yearly after the higher upfront, plus a battery-pack replacement in year three or four.
