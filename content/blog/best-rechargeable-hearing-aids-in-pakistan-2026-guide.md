Rechargeable hearing aids have gone from premium novelty to the default option in most price bands. The pitch is simple: put them in a case at night, take them out in the morning, never think about batteries again. The reality is also simple, with a few caveats that matter in Pakistan.

This guide covers how long they really last, what to check before you buy, and the cases where disposable batteries are still the better choice.

## How long do rechargeable hearing aids actually last?

Manufacturers claim 20 to 30 hours per charge. Real-world use, with Bluetooth streaming, is usually shorter:

| Use pattern | Expected runtime per charge |
| :--- | :--- |
| Normal conversation, no streaming | 18–24 hours |
| 2–3 hours of calls or TV streaming | 14–18 hours |
| Heavy streaming, all-day calls | 10–14 hours |
| Older cell (after 3–4 years) | 10–15% less |

Eighty percent of users charge overnight and never run out. If you work long shifts and stream heavily, ask for a model with a fast-charge option that gives several hours from a 30-minute top-up, and keep the case with you.

## What to check before buying

### 1. Charging case type

- **Standard case:** charges at home, needs a power point. Fine for most users.
- **Portable case with built-in battery:** gives two to four extra charges away from mains power. Worth paying for if you travel or face load shedding during your charging window.

### 2. Cell replacement policy

Lithium cells degrade. After three or four years the device will not hold a full day. Ask two questions: can the cell be replaced, and what does it cost? A model with a sealed, non-replaceable battery has a known expiry date.

### 3. Moisture and dust rating

Rechargeable aids are sealed more tightly than battery models, which helps in humid weather. Look for an IP68 rating if you are active or work outdoors. The rating covers sweat and rain, not swimming.

### 4. Charging contacts

Dirty contacts are the most common cause of "my aid didn't charge last night." The contacts are on the device and in the case. Wipe both with a dry cloth weekly. If your hands are stiff or your vision is weak, a case with magnetic drop-in slots is much easier than one that requires precise placement.

### 5. Heat

Charging generates heat and Pakistani summers add more. Charge on a shaded indoor surface, not in a parked car and not next to a window in direct afternoon sun.

## Rechargeable vs disposable batteries

| | Rechargeable | Disposable (size 10/312/13/675) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fuss | None once in routine | Change every 3–10 days |
| Cost over 5 years | Higher up front, lower ongoing | Lower up front, ongoing purchases |
| Runtime in remote areas | Needs a charging routine or portable case | Full runtime with a spare pack |
| Wet-weather habits | Sealed, better in humidity | Battery door is an entry point for moisture |
| Dexterity required | Minimal | Fine motor control, every few days |
| Best for | Most urban users | Long field postings, power cuts, very small in-canal models |

If you live somewhere with unpredictable power or travel where you cannot reliably charge, disposables are not old-fashioned; they are the practical answer. If you are in a city with a normal routine, rechargeable wins on convenience and on sealing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I leave them in the charger all night?

Yes. Charge circuits cut off when full, so overnight is fine and is the intended routine.

### Do they work with Bluetooth calls?

Most current models stream audio from iPhone and Android. Hands-free calling — where the aid microphones pick up your voice — is a premium-tier feature. Check the specific model if calls matter to you; see [Bluetooth hearing aids explained](/blog/bluetooth-hearing-aids-pakistan-guide).

### What happens during a power cut?

The aids themselves keep working until the charge runs out. The charger does not. A portable case with an internal battery covers this, and so does a small power bank with USB output.

### Are they waterproof?

No hearing aid is waterproof. IP68 means protected against dust and temporary immersion in fresh water, which is enough for sweat and rain. Take them out before swimming, showering, or wading.

## Verify the model, then verify the support

Two brands can sell the same core platform under different model names and different prices. Ask for the exact model number, confirm the cell replacement policy in writing, and ask who services the device locally.
