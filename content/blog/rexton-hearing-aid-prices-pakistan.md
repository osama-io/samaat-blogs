Rexton hearing aid price in Pakistan in 2026 ranges from about PKR 25,000 per ear for entry models to PKR 350,000 for the top Reach-platform devices. Rexton is the value brand of the major manufacturers in this market: comparable platform technology to its sister brands at visibly lower prices, which is why it dominates the PKR 25,000–90,000 band.

| Tier | Price per ear (PKR) | What you get |

| Entry digital BTE/ITE | 25,000 – 45,000 | Basic amplification tuned to your audiogram, manual programs |

Prices are per device (per ear). Fitting and some follow-ups are typically included — always get the included services in writing.

## Why Rexton buys you more rupee-for-rupee

Rexton shares technology platforms with Signia (both under the WS Audiology group) but imports and prices closer to the mid-market in Pakistan. Concretely: a PKR 80,000 Rexton mid-tier device usually offers the same channel count and connectivity tier that Signia labels at PKR 110,000–130,000. Trade-offs: fewer premium flagship features, a smaller accessories ecosystem, and less brand-driven demand on resale.

## Who should buy Rexton

1. First-time buyers who want programmable quality at the lowest genuine-device price (entry tier at PKR 25,000–45,000 — above amplifier territory).
2. Value-focused bilateral fittings: two Rexton mid-range devices at the total price of one Phonak premium.
3. Users who prefer replaceable-battery BTE simplicity over rechargeable premiums.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the Rexton hearing aid price in Pakistan in 2026?**
PKR 25,000–350,000 per ear. Entry digital models start at PKR 25,000; mid-range biX/Reach models with Bluetooth run PKR 70,000–150,000; the premium 3DX tier reaches PKR 350,000.

**Is Rexton a good brand or a cheap brand?**
A good brand, positioned value: it is owned by WS Audiology (the group behind Signia and Widex) and shares platforms with them. In Pakistan it is the strongest price-to-technology option below PKR 100,000 per ear.

**Rexton vs Signia — which should I buy?**
At the same tier, Rexton usually prices 20–30% lower with similar hardware. Choose Signia if you want the broader model range, Styletto form factors or a larger accessories ecosystem; choose Rexton for value.
