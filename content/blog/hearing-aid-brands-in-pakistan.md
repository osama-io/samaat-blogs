Seven hearing aid brands dominate Pakistan in 2026: Signia, Phonak, Rexton, Widex, Oticon, Starkey and Interton. All are genuine Western platform families; what differs is price positioning, import depth (model availability) and after-sales service in each city.

## How to pick a brand (in order)

1. **Pick the tier, not the brand** — your audiogram determines how much processing power you need; see [types and costs](/blog/hearing-aid-types-and-costs-pakistan).
2. **Within the tier, compare on service**: warranty length, local repair turnaround, included follow-ups. A brand with a service partner in your city beats a "better" brand serviced by courier.
3. **Then compare models** on features you will actually use (Bluetooth, rechargeable, telecoil for mosque/auditorium use).

## Brand-city availability notes

- **Lahore**: all seven brands importable, densest distributor network — best place to compare quotes.
- **Karachi**: full Signia/Phonak ranges; other brands vary by clinic.
- **Islamabad/Rawalpindi**: strong audiology clinics carrying Signia, Phonak, Widex.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which hearing aid brands are available in Pakistan?**
Signia, Phonak, Rexton, Widex, Oticon, Starkey and Interton, all genuine imports. Signia and Rexton have the widest dealer coverage; Oticon and Starkey concentrate in major-city clinics.

**Which brand is the most reliable in Pakistan?**
Signia and Rexton lead on service-network depth — the practical definition of reliable here. Phonak and Widex devices have excellent hardware reliability; their limitation is how many clinics stock and service them.

**What is the cheapest genuine hearing aid brand in Pakistan?**
Interton and Rexton basics start at PKR 15,000–25,000 per ear — genuine programmable devices, above the amplifier tier. Anything much cheaper in the market is an amplifier, not an aid.
