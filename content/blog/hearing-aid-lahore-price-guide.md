> Updated September 2026 with current Lahore market quotes. Prices per ear.

Hearing aid price in Lahore in 2026 ranges from PKR 15,000 per ear for entry digital devices to PKR 550,000 for flagship Phonak and Signia platforms. Lahore is Pakistan's most competitive hearing-aid market — the densest distributor network in the country — so with the right quotes you pay about 5–10% less than Islamabad or smaller cities for the same device.

| Tier | Price per ear (PKR) | Typical models in Lahore stock |

Lahore's usually-cited mid-range pair (both ears, fitted, follow-ups included): **PKR 140,000–300,000 total**.

## Where the stock and fittings are

The core of Lahore's hearing-aid trade sits around Hall Road / Mall Road medical suppliers plus dedicated audiology clinics in Gulberg, DHA and Johar Town. Audiology-built clinics (with an in-house audiologist and testing booth) are the better fit for first-time buyers; distributor counters are better for a known model at the best price. Either way, get fittings and trial period in writing — Lahore's pricing advantage matters most when service is included.

## How to compare two Lahore quotes

1. Same model number and platform generation on both quotes.
2. Per ear or per pair — Lahore quotes use both conventions.
3. Included services: audiogram, fitting, follow-ups (how many), warranty length.
4. Genuine-import check: serial number and warranty registration.

For most Lahore buyers, a mid-range RIC from Signia or Rexton at PKR 70,000–150,000 per ear is the honest sweet spot: full digital programs, Bluetooth, rechargeable options, and the city's best service coverage. Pay Phonak/Widex premiums for meeting-room noise, severe losses or pediatric fittings.
