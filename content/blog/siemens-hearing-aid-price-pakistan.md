## Why the name changed, and why it matters to your wallet

Signia is not a knock-off of Siemens. It is the direct successor: same German factories at Erlangen and Pleszew heritage, same audiologist tooling, renamed in 2016 after Siemens sold its audiology business into the Sivantos/WS Audiology group. In Pakistan that matters because the second-hand market still trades on the Siemens name. A seller shouting "Siemens, only 25,000 rupees" is describing one of three things:

1. Old Siemens stock, likely 5+ years old, no active warranty, parts multiples slower.
2. A Singapore-assembled copy sold under a near-identical name.
3. A China-copy board tuned to "sound loud" rather than to your audiogram.

## Where to buy genuine Signia, safely

Three channels operate in Pakistan, and their risk profiles are not equal:

1. **Authorised dealers.** Slower paperwork, but your warranty ticket is real. This is where in-country Signia service in 5 to 7 working days actually applies. Ask for the importer's name on the paperwork.
2. **Unofficial importers and bazaar shops.** Often genuine hardware bought from grey import, sold cheaper. The device may be original; the warranty ticket often is not, and service route becomes informal.
3. **Online listings and Marketplace.** The cheapest prices, the highest share of copies and old stock. Our [Daraz, OLX vs dealer comparison](/blog/hearing-aids-daraz-olx-vs-dealer-pakistan) runs the numbers on why the discount is smaller than it looks once repairs and disputes are counted.

A practical test that works in Pakistan: ask the dealer for the exact model number and the importer service point before payment. A genuine dealer answers in one sentence, and the answer is verifiable. A copy seller changes the subject or names a non-existent model.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Siemens still selling hearing aids?

No. Siemens sold its hearing instrument business and the devices are now made and sold as Signia. Same German engineering heritage and factories, new name. Every "Siemens" hearing aid quoted to you today is either old stock, a copy, or simply being called the wrong thing by the seller.

### What is the Siemens (Signia) hearing aid price in Pakistan?

PKR 55,000 to 380,000 per ear for genuine Signia devices in 2026. Entry super-power BTE sits at the bottom of that band, premium Styletto and Insio at the top, with the popular mid tier around PKR 110,000 to 180,000.

### Is a "Siemens" hearing aid from OLX or a bazaar genuine?

Almost never at the quoted price. A real old Siemens device 5 or more years old would have no active warranty and no parts support in Pakistan. See the price ranges above: anything dramatically below PKR 55,000 that claims to be Siemens is a copy. Compare with the [Daraz-OLX vs dealer guide](/blog/hearing-aids-daraz-olx-vs-dealer-pakistan).

### How long do Signia hearing aids last in Pakistan?

Five to seven years of daily wear with clean habits and dry storage. Heat and humidity are the main killers here, so read the [care guide](/blog/how-to-care-for-your-hearing-aids) before your first pair. In-warranty Signia service runs 5 to 7 days per repair.
