## Why A&M Exists and What Genuine at Rs 8,000 Means

Pakistan has a large domestic market of hearing devices sold in bazaars, pharmacies, and online for Rs 1,500-15,000. Most are amplifiers - fixed-gain boxes that make everything louder. They cannot be programmed to an audiogram, they distort, and they make mild-to-moderate losses worse over years of use.

A&M is the local answer to that market: assembled in Pakistan, CE certified, and sold through the same clinic channel as imported brands. That means:

- **Genuine digital processing**, not a linear amplifier, at Rs 8,000 for basic models.
- **Audiogram programming included** in the fitting, so the device actually matches your PTA result rather than a generic curve.
- **A price floor** at roughly one-third of the cheapest imported genuine device, because there is no import duty, shipping agent, or currency chain stacked on top.

The overall Pakistani hearing aid market runs Rs 15,000-550,000+ per ear, and imported brands start near Rs 18,000 (Interton) at the genuine entry level. A&M sits below all of them entirely.

## Who A&M Suits Best - and Who It Does Not

A&M is the right call for:

- **Tight budgets** where Rs 35,000 per ear (Rs 70,000 for two) is already a stretch.
- **Mild to moderate hearing loss** with a fairly flat audiogram, where a modest channel count is genuinely sufficient.
- **Second-ear pairing** where one ear already has a premium aid and the second needs something functional rather than identical.
- **A backup set** for a premium user who wants a spare for travel, dust exposure, or monsoon months.

A&M is the wrong call for:

- **Steep or ski-slope hearing loss** that needs many narrow channels shaped precisely.
- **Heavy workplace noise** (shops, classrooms, generators, rooster markets) where premium noise management earns its Rs 150,000+ price.
- **Long-term premium expectations** like Bluetooth streaming, app control, or rechargeable lithium batteries - none of that is A&M territory.

There is a clean handoff point. At Rs 25,000-180,000, Rexton (SWS/WS Audiology family, shared heritage with Signia) gives you a stronger mid-range workhorse with wider dealer support.

## Where to Buy A&M - and How to Avoid Amplifier Scams

The Rs 20,000 line matters to repeat: below that, many shops in Pakistan market amplifiers as "hearing aids", especially on marketplaces and general-store counters. To avoid buying a Rs 2,000 amplifier labelled as a genuine Aid:

1. Ask explicitly: "Is this programmable to my audiogram, and is fitting included?"
2. Ask for the make/model in writing and check the serial number warranty.
3. Get the PTA test done first (Rs 500-2,000, air and bone conduction; tympanometry extra) - a clinic that skips the test before fitting is not a clinic to trust.
4. Reject any device that cannot be reprogrammed later as your hearing changes.

If a seller quotes below Rs 8,000 for a genuine "A&M or imported hearing aid", walk away - that price is only plausible for an amplifier, not a programmable device.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is A&M hearing aid really genuine?

Yes. A&M is manufactured in Pakistan and carries CE certification for its hearing aid products. It is fitted through the same audiology clinic channel imported brands use, which is what separates it from the Rs 1,500-15,000 amplifiers sold in general stores and online.

### Why is A&M so cheap compared to imported brands?

Because there is no import duty, international shipping currency-chain, or distributor margin stacked on top the way imported brands have. At Rs 8,000-35,000 per ear, A&M is the cheapest genuine option in the market, which runs overall from Rs 15,000 to Rs 550,000+ per ear.

### How long does an A&M hearing aid last?

Expect roughly 5-7 years of usable life in Pakistan, matching the norm for any brand here - heat, humidity and sweat shorten device life compared to the 7-10 years often quoted abroad. Care routines (dry box, wax guards, regular cleaning) matter more to lifespan than the brand on the shell.

### Are A&M hearing aids digital?

Yes, throughout the current lineup. Even entry models at Rs 8,000 have digital compression and programmable fitting. The analogue/amplifier tier in Pakistan exists below roughly Rs 20,000, and A&M is deliberately priced to pull genuine digital into that same budget bracket.

### What is A&M's warranty?

Warranty follows the standard 12-24 months manufacturer pattern, with the serial number registered at fitting. Confirm the exact term in writing before purchase, since warranty terms can vary by clinic and by model tier even within the same brand.
