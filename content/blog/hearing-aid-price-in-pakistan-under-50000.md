Genuine hearing aids in Pakistan under Rs 50,000 per ear do exist: the A&M line (made in Pakistan) starts at Rs 8,000 and Interton's entry models start around Rs 18,000, with Rexton and Unitron entry tiers dipping to about Rs 25,000–30,000 on dealer quotes. Below roughly Rs 20,000 the market shifts from programmable hearing aids to simple amplifiers — you must know which you are buying before you pay.

Rs 50,000 per ear is the ceiling of the value segment, and it is the segment where the most money is wasted. This guide maps exactly what each band buys, which brands play in it, and how to spot the amplifier listings that crowd every marketplace.

## What genuine under-Rs-50,000 options look like (2026)

Per ear; double for a pair; fitting and audiogram programming normally included by a dealer.

| Band (PKR per ear) | Brands on dealer shelves | Technology you get | Honest limits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8,000 – 15,000 | A&M basic digital BTE | Digital processing, 2–4 channels, manual programs | Quiet rooms first; limited noise handling; 12-month warranty |

Aid-for-aid, these are not placebo products. A Rs 25,000 Rexton or Interton programmed to your audiogram is a genuine medical device with a serial number and warranty — the difference to a Rs 250,000 aid is feature depth: channels, noise algorithms, streaming, binaural coordination.

## The amplifier trap: what sits below Rs 20,000 on every marketplace

Listings at Rs 1,499–15,000 on Daraz, OLX and in some shops sell **sound amplifiers**, not hearing aids:

- Fixed amplification — no programming, no audiogram, same boost for a 40 dB loss and an 80 dB loss.
- No feedback cancellation — they whistle in real ears.
- No serial number, no warranty path, no service.
- Often referred to politely as "first copy" or "China original" by resellers who know exactly what they are.

Wearing an amplifier tuned wrongly for months can make reaching a good fit later harder, because the brain adapts to distorted input and speech-rehabilitation starts from further back. The saving of Rs 20,000 is the most expensive economy in this market.

If your budget truly caps at Rs 8,000–15,000, buy the genuine A&M instead — a Pakistani-made, CE-certified digital hearing aid fitted by an audiologist. Read the dedicated [A&M price guide](/blog/am-hearing-aid-price-in-pakistan-cheapest-genuine) before shopping anywhere else.

## What a pair costs, and whether you can afford two

The under-50k segment is where genuine two-ear fittings become affordable for the first time:

| Choice | Pair cost (PKR) | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| One A&M mid (approx 15,000) | 15,000 | One aided ear; see [one vs two aids](/blog/one-vs-two-hearing-aids-bilateral-pakistan) |
| Two A&M mid | 30,000 | Binaural hearing — localisation, speech in noise |
| Two Interton entry | 36,000 – 50,000 | Better clarity, app control on some models |
| One premium (70,000+) | 70,000+ | More features, but one-sided hearing |

For most bilateral losses, two basic programmed aids beat one good aid worn alone — the brain does the work in stereo. This is the strongest argument against "save up for one expensive aid".

## What a dealer must give you at this budget

The under-50k buyer is the main target of bait pricing, so check every one of these before paying:

1. **PTA hearing test included or Rs 500–2,000** — programming without an audiogram is not a fitting.
2. **Written model name and serial number** on the receipt — this is what makes warranty real.
3. **12-month minimum warranty** with repair or replace terms spelled out.
4. **Trial window** — even 7 days makes a huge difference at this price point.
5. **Battery type stated** — size 13 orange (7–10 days) is common in BTEs at this tier; a 6-pack runs Rs 350–650.

If any of those five is missing, walk out; whatever is being sold is not a genuine aid with support. Our [complete buying guide](/blog/complete-guide-to-hearing-aids-in-pakistan) covers the full checklist.

## Where prices dip further

- **Lahore** runs the densest distributor market in Pakistan — mid-range aids commonly quote 5–10% lower there; see the [Lahore price guide](/blog/hearing-aid-lahore-price-guide).
- **Older-generation stock** (previous platform years) is discounted 20–40% at clearance. A discontinued-tier Rexton programmed to your audiogram is still a genuine aid, often the best value in the building.
- **Left/right mismatch sales** — a single register-match unit from a pair display sometimes discounts heavily.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest genuine hearing aid in Pakistan?

The A&M brand, made in Pakistan, starts around Rs 8,000 per ear from dealers who fit and programme it to your audiogram. It is a CE-certified digital hearing aid, not an amplifier. Anything cheaper is a sound amplifier and can harm your remaining hearing comfort.

### Is a Rs 25,000 hearing aid good enough?

For quiet homes, TV, one-to-one talk and small shops, a Rs 25,000–35,000 programmed aid from Rexton/Interton/Unitron is genuinely adequate for mild to moderate loss. It will struggle in wedding halls and busy markets — that is where the Rs 100,000+ tiers earn their price.

### Can I buy a genuine hearing aid from Daraz under Rs 50,000?

No genuine programmable hearing aid is sold new on Daraz/OLX in this range; what you find is amplifiers and "first copy" units with no warranty. Buy from a dealer who prints the serial number on your receipt, or see our [Daraz/OLX vs dealer comparison](/blog/hearing-aids-daraz-olx-vs-dealer-pakistan).

### Do cheaper aids have worse batteries?

Battery size and drain depend on technology level, not price tier. Entry BTEs at this budget usually take size 13 (7–10 days per cell), costing Rs 350–650 per 6-pack — running cost is similar to premium aids. See [battery prices in Pakistan](/blog/hearing-aid-battery-price-pakistan).

### Can I upgrade later without losing money?

Mostly no — hearing aids are not trade-in assets in Pakistan; resale value is poor. If an upgrade in 2–3 years is likely, buy at the Rs 25,000–35,000 band rather than stretching to 50,000 now, and keep the older pair as a backup set afterwards.

### Are rechargeable aids available under Rs 50,000?

Not genuinely. Rechargeable systems start meaningfully higher (Rs 120,000+ per ear); anything rechargeable listed under Rs 50,000 is a low-end charger circuit with a fixed amplifier. This is covered fully in [rechargeable vs battery aids](/blog/rechargeable-vs-battery-hearing-aids).
